When an eyeless family staggers out of Wulsvil Forest, Everett Dwyer assumes a wild beast took their eyes. He soon learns a worse truth: they blinded themselves to escape something coming. Something they will not name.

The only clue is a dead word. It survives in the town’s old black ledger, yet no one can tell Everett what it means. It belongs to no language. It has no history. It is as though the world agreed, all at once, to forget.

That word is Religion.

The answer to what is coming lies in the word the world destroyed. Everett must recover its meaning before the Expatria burn him with it.

The World of Gyra

The world that agreed to forget

Gyra is a world shaped as much by what has been erased as by what remains. Its cities, forests and old roads stand upon a history no living person can fully explain. Beyond the accepted story, the Expatria preserve fragments of truths the world once chose to destroy.

Everett’s search begins with a single dead word, but the answer reaches across Gyra. Old divisions are tightening, forgotten powers are stirring, and something the eyeless refuse to name is drawing closer.

SBK

The author who created Gyra and The Burned Name

Old-school fantasy with modern pace

Sean Bobby Kerr is an Australian fantasy author writing large-scale stories about memory, belief, power and the truths people choose to bury. The Maelum Trilogy combines the atmosphere, scope and immersion of classic epic fantasy with the momentum of modern storytelling.

He writes from Sydney and is currently bringing Everett’s journey through Gyra to completion.

Long before Gyra had a name, Sean was drawing its coastline in the margins of school notebooks. The Maelum Trilogy grew out of a question that never quite left him: what does a society do with the truths it can’t afford to keep?
